# README.md: protocol and supporting commands, and the library's features

README.md lists the protocol commands, linked to PROTOCOL.md, apart
from the supporting commands, and takes from SPEC.md what belongs to
the library: the routing rules, the agents' worktree steps, the prompt
templates and the changelog's reading.

Effects:

- the command table: "Protocol commands" (the fourteen, each linked to
  its PROTOCOL.md section) and "Supporting commands" (list, show,
  status, task list, task show, patch show, changelog, route, install,
  serve), each with a line saying what the group is
- Routing gives the rules table from SPEC.md section 10
- Agents gives the worktree steps from SPEC.md section 11
- Templates lists the prompt templates and their values from SPEC.md
  section 4
- Changelog gives the reading from SPEC.md section 9
- every link to SPEC.md points to PROTOCOL.md or to a README section
